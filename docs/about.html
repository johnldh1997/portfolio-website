Background

I'm Donghyeon Lee (John Lee), a Computer Science graduate from Auckland University of Technology, majoring in Software Development. I've built everything from cross-platform mobile apps to automated data pipelines, and I like projects with a clear before-and-after — messy data turned into a clean dashboard, a manual process turned into an automated one. Outside of tech, I'm fluent in both English and Korean, and I hold a full driver's licence.

Currently focused on

Right now I'm deepening my Power BI and SQL skills through self-directed projects — building end-to-end pipelines like the European Bike Sales dashboard and the NZD Exchange Rate Archive, from raw data through to an executive-ready report. Each one has been a chance to get sharper with DAX, data modelling, and thinking about what a business actually needs to see, not just what's technically interesting to build.

What I'm looking for

I'm looking for a data analyst, junior developer, or related role where I can keep building on this — ideally in a team where I can contribute from day one while continuing to learn. I care more about curiosity and initiative in a workplace than having every box already ticked.
